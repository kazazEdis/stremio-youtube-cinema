Picking more than one

Each country is its own addon and Stremio merges them, so installing two gives you the films either one can play, with every copy of a film offered together. Install them one after another — they sit side by side rather than replacing each other.

Why the lists differ

A rights holder can allow an upload in some countries and not others. Choosing your country adds the films it lets you see and never adds one you cannot play. Where a film has several copies on YouTube all of them are offered, sharpest first, with anything needing a YouTube sign-in last.
